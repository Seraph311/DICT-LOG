## Design Context

### Users
Provincial office reviewers and administrators use the dashboard at desks to understand visitor activity and CSM results. Visitors complete the public survey on their own phones, including small 360px-wide screens.

### Brand Personality
Civic, clear, dependable. The interface should feel calm, trustworthy, efficient, and respectful of public-service work.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined civic editorial: a light-first experience using the existing DICT blue and gold palette, with restrained hierarchy and readable data visualization. Avoid neon, decorative gradients, and visual noise.

### Design Principles
- Make operational data scannable at a glance, then progressively reveal detail.
- Use clear labels, strong contrast, and keyboard-friendly controls throughout.
- Keep public forms welcoming and practical on narrow mobile screens.
- Treat privacy as part of the visual language: show only the aggregate data each role needs.
- Support reduced motion and communicate loading, empty, error, and success states plainly.
